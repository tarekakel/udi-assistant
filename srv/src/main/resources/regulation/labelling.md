# UDI on the label and packaging (EU MDR 2017/745, Article 27(3) and Annex VI Part C)

## UDI carrier placement
The UDI carrier must be placed on the label of the device and on all higher levels of packaging. Shipping containers are exempt: a shipping container is not considered a higher level of packaging. Where the device or its packaging is too small, the UDI may be placed on the next higher packaging level.

## AIDC and HRI
The UDI carrier includes the UDI in two forms: an AIDC (Automatic Identification and Data Capture) representation such as a linear barcode, a 2D barcode (for example GS1 DataMatrix) or RFID, and an HRI (Human Readable Interpretation) of the same characters. If there is a significant constraint limiting the use of both AIDC and HRI on the label, only the AIDC format is required; devices intended for use outside healthcare facilities, such as home-care devices, must carry the HRI even if that means omitting the AIDC.

## Direct marking of reusable devices
Reusable devices that require cleaning, disinfection, sterilisation or refurbishing between uses must bear the UDI directly on the device itself (direct marking), in addition to the label. Direct marking is not required where it would interfere with the safety or performance of the device or where the device cannot be marked because it is not technically feasible.

## UDI-PI content on the label
The UDI-PI on the label carries the production information relevant to the device: the lot or batch number, the serial number, the software identification and the manufacturing or expiry date where these appear on the label. For software, the software identification is considered the UDI-PI.
