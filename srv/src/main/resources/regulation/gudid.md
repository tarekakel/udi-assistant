# FDA GUDID (United States, 21 CFR Part 830)

## What GUDID holds
The Global Unique Device Identification Database (GUDID) is the FDA database that contains key device identification information for devices marketed in the United States. It stores the device identifier (DI) portion of the UDI and associated attributes. Production identifiers (lot, serial, expiry) are not submitted to GUDID; they are only on the label.

## Who submits
The labeler, usually the manufacturer or the brand owner whose name appears on the label, is responsible for submitting the device record to GUDID. Records must be submitted before the device is marketed, and updates must be made when an attribute changes.

## Differences from EUDAMED
GUDID has no equivalent of the Basic UDI-DI; the primary DI plays a comparable role for grouping packaging levels. The EU UDI-DI and the US DI can be the same GTIN when the same issuing agency is used, but the regulatory records are separate and must be maintained in both databases. Attribute sets differ between the two databases, so a change may require a new record in one jurisdiction but not the other.
