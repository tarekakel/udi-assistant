# EUDAMED registration (EU MDR 2017/745, Articles 29 and 30)

## Registration before placing on the market
Before placing a device on the market, the manufacturer assigns a Basic UDI-DI to the device and provides it, together with the other core data elements listed in Annex VI Part B, to the UDI database in EUDAMED. Each UDI-DI of the device is also registered. Systems and procedure packs are registered by the natural or legal person who assembles them.

## Keeping the record current
The manufacturer must keep the information in the UDI database up to date. Changes to a data element that does not require a new UDI-DI must be entered within 30 days of the change. Where a change results in a new UDI-DI, the new UDI-DI is registered as a new device record.

## Actor registration and SRN
Before registering devices, the manufacturer, authorised representative and importer register as economic operators in EUDAMED and receive a Single Registration Number (SRN). The SRN is required when applying to a notified body for conformity assessment and is referenced in device registrations.

## Class I devices and the database
Manufacturers of class I devices (other than sterile, measuring or reusable surgical class I devices) draw up the EU declaration of conformity themselves, but they are still required to assign a Basic UDI-DI and to register the device in the UDI database before placing it on the market.
